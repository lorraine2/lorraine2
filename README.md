## Jonathan Lorraine

I'm a Senior Research Scientist at NVIDIA Research in Jan Kautz's Learning and Perception Research (LPR) group, working on agentic AI for science and engineering. My research spans rewards, verifiers, and evaluation for self-improving systems; world models and generative models for 3D and video; and scalable nested optimization. I completed my PhD in machine learning at the University of Toronto, advised by David Duvenaud. Before NVIDIA, I worked at Google and Meta AI.
