## Jonathan Lorraine

Research Scientist at **NVIDIA** in the [Spatial Intelligence Lab](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/sil/) with Sanja Fidler, working on **generative physical AI**. I completed my PhD in machine learning at the University of Toronto in 2024, advised by David Duvenaud. My research centers on **ultra-scalable nested optimization** — building the tools that power modern AI models and applying them to multimodal generation, hyperparameter tuning, and learning in games. Previously: production AutoML at Google, and multi-agent learning at Facebook (Meta) AI Research with Jakob Foerster.
